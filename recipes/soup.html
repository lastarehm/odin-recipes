Soup!!!

This is the recipe for really awesome soup. You throw a bunch of stuff in a pot and then you boil for like an hour. I eat it all the time. It's awesome.

Ingredients:

  • brown lentils
  • carrots
  • celery
  • onion
  • bayleaf
  • cumin
  • garlic

Optional:

  • yogurt
  • cilantro

Instructions:

  1. Chop the onion, carrot, and onion
  2. Saute the onion
  3. Add garlic and cumin and saute some more
  4. Add lentils and broth
  5. Simmer until lentils are soft (30 min maybe?)
