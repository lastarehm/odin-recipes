Delicious things of different flavors and textures between pieces of bread

Ingredients:

  • bread
  • avocado
  • portabella mushroom
  • cheddar cheese
  • tomato (super fresh)
  • mayonnaise

Instructions

  1. Saute mushroom
  2. Toast bread
  3. Apply mayo to bread
  4. Add avocado slices to bread
  5. Add other stuff to bread
  6. Salt tomato slice gently
  7. Add tomato to bread
  8. Place bread on top of other bread and stuff
  9. Eat sandwich
